# Third-party rights and public source boundary

The MIT license covers original COC orchestration, documentation and designed project graphics. Third-party tools, marks, licenses and support obligations remain with their publishers.

No vendor executables/runtime libraries, upstream extractor source, ISOs, VM/container images, signatures/symbol bundles or private evidence/transcripts are distributed. Tool names, official pointers and immutable image digests document packages obtained separately for the private installed toolkit. The three small response fixtures are project-created harmless synthetic test data; they contain no functional malware.

The shared emblem and menu convention follow the author's COC/USB #1 interface. Referenced projects include osquery, Dissect, Zimmerman tools, Sysinternals, Ghidra, PyInstaller Extractor NG, YARA-related projects, Zeek, Suricata, Plaso, SIFT and REMnux. Free availability does not imply every package may be redistributed or used under MIT terms. Review current publisher licenses before obtaining or using any dependency.
